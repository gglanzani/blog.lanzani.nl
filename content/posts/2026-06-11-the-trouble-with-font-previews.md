Marcin Wichary [blogged] an extensive follow-up to my [post] from yesterday and I found it a fascinating read with a path forward for Redmond to fix their design.

It turns out (as always) that the problem is not as easy to fix (apparently [Typography is impossible](https://medium.engineering/typography-is-impossible-5872b0c7f891)), but, if Microsoft wanted an easy way out, they could turn off the preview once a replacement font has been set.
