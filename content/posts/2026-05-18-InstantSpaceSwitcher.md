I keep [Fantastical] and [Things] on my second macOS space, and everything else on the first.

However, there's a bug that showed a black screen for a split second every time I would switch space using the trackpad.

After searching how to disable the animation, I stumbled upon this neat little utility that does exactly that and it is glorious.
